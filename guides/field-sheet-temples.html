Field Sheet 03: Shrines and Temples

Print this sheet double-sided on US Letter (flip on long edge), laminate it, and carry a dry-erase marker. The front is what you need at the gate: the map, the locations, and the rules. The back is the shot list and the shot log. It supplements the Stock Footage in-class assignment and the Pilgrimage Shots Location Plan add-on; it does not replace them.

Etiquette and rules

  • Bow at the gate. Walk to the side; the center is for the deity.
  • Shrine (torii): bow twice, clap twice, bow once. Temple (gate, incense): bow, do not clap.
  • Never film people praying from the front, or inside a hall. Obey every 撮影禁止 sign.
  • No tripods, drones, flash, or reflectors. Never photograph priests, shrine maidens, or worshippers without asking. Quiet voices.

Pick one location

1

Suga Shrine, Yotsuya 須賀神社

The stairs with the red handrail from the last scene of Your Name. Residential street: quiet voices.

Yotsuya-sanchome Station (Marunouchi Line), about 5 min.

2

Hie Shrine, Akasaka 日枝神社

6:00 to 17:00. A red torii stairway; office towers behind the roofs. Best: morning.

Tameike-sanno Station, Exit 7, about 3 min; or Akasaka Station (Chiyoda Line), about 3 min.

3

Nezu Shrine, Bunkyo 根津神社

Karamon gate closes 18:00 Mar–Sep, 17:00 Nov–Jan. Edo-period buildings; a tunnel of small red torii.

Nezu or Sendagi Station (Chiyoda Line), or Todaimae (Namboku Line), about 5 min each.

4

Senso-ji, Asakusa 浅草寺

Five minutes beyond Kaminarimon. Main hall 6:00 to 17:00 (from 6:30 Oct–Mar). No photos or video inside the main hall. Avoid Sanja Matsuri weekend (third weekend of May).

Asakusa Station (Ginza Line), Exit 1, about 5 min.

Settings

  • Landscape, always. A Reel gets cropped to 9:16 in the edit.
  • Highest quality your camera or phone offers, 24 or 30 fps. Slow motion for smoke or water if it has one.
  • Lock exposure on the gate, not the bright sky behind it.
  • Brace on a pillar, never a lantern or statue. Roll 3 s before and after every shot.

The nine shots

A · Establishing / proof

1The gate. Torii or temple gate, wide, centered, level, locked off. 15 s. (Read 1.4.4)
2Proof. The stone name marker or the plaque on the gate. 10 s.

B · Geography / connector

3The approach. Walk the path or climb the stairs slowly, from the side. 15 s.
4The threshold. Frame the grounds through the gate, then hold. 15 s. (Read 2.5)

C · Texture

5Low angle up. From the bottom stair or the base of a gate, tilt up. 15 s. (Read 2.3.1)
6Details. Ema plaques, omikuji, water basin, incense smoke. 10 s each.
7Old against new. A roof or gate in front, office towers behind, in one frame. 15 s. (Read 1.6)

D · Human rhythm

8Ritual, from behind. Hands at the basin, a bow at a distance. Backs only. 10 s.
9Time passing. Locked frame of the approach; people enter, cross, and leave; hold the empty path. 30 s. (Read 3.3.3)

Audio pass

  • 60-second wild track in the grounds, away from the path. Everyone silent (2.4.1 room tone).
  • Two clips, 20 to 30 s for the Sound Log: gravel footsteps at Close (under 1 m), and one ritual sound at Mid (1 to 5 m): rope bell, coins, two claps. Tag Shrine.
  • No prayers or private conversations on your recording.

Pilgrimage Shots option

Only if your group chose the Pilgrimage Shots Location Plan add-on. Your Name (2016): the Suga Shrine stairs in the last scene. On-site anchor: “Stair landing level.” Keep the red handrail, the stair edges, and the street below in frame.

Where your footage goes

  • Stock Footage tab: 3 to 5 clips, 10 to 20 s each. Sound Log form: the two clips.
  • Pilgrimage Shots Location Plan form (if chosen): the Suga Shrine frame and shots 1 and 2.
  • Location Mini-Doc / Tokyo Reel: the “Shrine / temple / historical layer” anchor.
