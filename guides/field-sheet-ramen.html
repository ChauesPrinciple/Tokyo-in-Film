Rules

  • Ask before you film inside: “Satsuei shite mo ii desu ka?” A no, or a no-photo sign, means phone away.
  • No tripods, flash, or lights. No faces without a yes; hands and bowls are fine.
  • Never hold a seat or the line to film.
  • Film fast, then eat. Hours change: check the ramen map before you travel.

The nine shots, every shop

A · Establishing / proof

1The front. Noren and sign from across the street, wide, locked off, name readable. 10 s. (Read 1.5)

B · Geography / connector

2The queue. The line from behind, or your feet moving up it. 10 s.
3The ticket machine. Your hand, the button, the ticket. No machine? The menu. 10 s.

C · Texture: the Tampopo lesson

4The whole bowl. Overhead or 45°, before you touch it. Study the arrangement. 10 s.
5The stroke. Chopstick tips across the surface. 5 s.
6The pork. Close-up. Apologize to it (off camera, please). 5 s.
7The lift. Noodles up through the steam, slow motion if you have it. 5 s.

D · Human rhythm: the Ramen Heads lesson

8The hands. Only after a yes: the chef repeating one task, from your seat. 15 s.
9The finish. The empty bowl back up on the counter. 5 s.

Settings

  • Landscape, highest quality. Grid and level on.
  • Wipe the lens before shot 4. Steam fogs it.
  • Lock focus and exposure on the bowl. Steam shows best against a dark background, lit from the side.
  • Same height, same angle for shots 4 to 7 at every shop, so the twelve bowls cut together. (Same tasks, every day: Ramen Heads again.)

Audio pass

  • The slurp. Your own, 10 s, phone on the counter. (Slurping is polite here. Commit.)
  • Two clips, 20 to 30 s for the Sound Log: the kitchen, and one detail (the greeting, a bowl set down).
  • Shop radio is copyrighted: archive yes, Reel music no. No strangers’ voices up close.

Where your footage goes

  • Location Mini-Doc: a shop is a location; the nine shots fill establishing, geography, texture, and human rhythm.
  • Location Sound Log form: the two clips.
  • Your bowl sequence: shot 4 from every shop, in the order you ate them.
