# Metaphysics Audit — LONGSHOT.txt

**Audit scope:** every line referring to the entity, the outside, observation, the pull, or the eating. Each flagged against the locked rule set.

**Current locked rule set:**

1. **Ito's proof derives only two things:** the universe has an outside; observation-dependent horizons are bidirectional (observation reaches the outside; the outside can reach back). The proof does NOT derive creature behavior, ecology, pulling, or feeding.
2. **The predator, listening, pulling, and eating are Tanaka's inferences** from the proof combined with the sector-nine signal data. Whenever a character cites "the proof" for anything beyond bidirectionality, that is a violation.
3. **The entity:** higher-dimensional predator. Shark/whale metaphor. Eats universes as mass. Life is krill — the concentrated part that gets pulled toward it first.
4. **Banned entity-verbs:** aware, decide, want, need, try (as mental effort), choose, intend, seek, communicate, understand, offer, listen (when it means mental perception), agree, perceive (mental), malevolent, benevolent, wait (for us).
5. **Approved entity-verbs:** feeds, grazes, moves, swims, follows, tracks, is drawn to, arrives, crosses, bites, pulls, hooks, reels, chews.
6. **We are not the object of a hunt.** We are the signal it registers. We happen to be a concentration of coherence at the spot its baleen is closing on.
7. **Two mechanisms:** background eating of universes (ongoing, indifferent); active pulling of living/coherent patches toward the mouth (triggered by sensory registration of coherence).
8. **Register:** proof inserts in mathematical register (no pulp imagery). Dialogue allows metaphor — Tanaka explaining to Marshall can reach for shark, whale, baleen, hook, line, reel.

## Violations found (by severity)

### HIGH — contradicts locked rule, must fix

**L15 (Logline 1).** `"the universe has an outside, and it perceives back through the instrument built to see it."`
- **Problem:** "perceives back" is mental-state verb on the entity.
- **Fix:** match the proof insert — `"reaches back through the instrument built to see it."`

**L17 (Logline 2).** `"What is on the other side was Dr. Hiroshi Ito's life's work, forty years ago, and it has been waiting for someone to finish his proof."`
- **Problem:** "has been waiting" gives the entity intention and patience across time. Violates entity-verb ban.
- **Fix:** rewrite to remove entity agency. E.g., `"What is on the other side was Dr. Hiroshi Ito's life's work, forty years ago. Nobody finished his proof in time."`

**L407–408 (array-room Tanaka to speaker).** `"You're not him. But you know what he knew."`
- **Problem:** "you know what he knew" is a knowledge-possession claim about the entity. Mental-state. Violates ban.
- **Fix:** Tanaka's line needs to be about the entity's effect, not its mind. E.g., `"You're not him. But what he proved is what I am reading through you."` — or cut the second line entirely.

**L547 (Tanaka to Marshall, main revelation).** `"The proof derives what's on the other side of a horizon. Not vacuum. An ecology. Things live there. Universes are what they eat."`
- **Problem:** The proof does NOT derive ecology. This is Tanaka's inference from proof + sector-nine data. By sourcing it to "the proof," Tanaka is over-claiming Ito's math.
- **Fix:** Split the claim. The proof derives bidirectionality; Tanaka's reading of the signal data plus the proof lets him infer the ecology. Rewrite so Tanaka cites the right source for the right claim.

**L1052 (Carter's self-enucleation rationale).** `"The proof says observation is what it pulls against. The sharper the observation, the stronger the grip."`
- **Problem:** Same violation. The proof says observation is bidirectional; it does NOT say anything about "pulling against." That's Tanaka's later inference. Carter is citing the proof for something the proof doesn't prove.
- **Fix:** Carter should cite Tanaka's synthesis, not Ito's proof. E.g., `"Tanaka's reading says observation is what it pulls against..."` or reframe as Carter's own inference.

**L1320 (Nakamura, pre-relay-destruction).** `"Not equipment that was listening."`
- **Problem:** "listening" as entity-mental-state verb. Banned.
- **Fix:** `"Not equipment that was carrying something."` or `"Not equipment that was an open channel."`

### MEDIUM — borderline, resolve one way or the other

**L750 (Tanaka's "He was right" moment).** `"What lives in it gets pulled toward the mouth first. Something has been pulling us for forty-seven days. We are almost there."`
- **Note:** "mouth" is the cartoon body-part we rejected from the proof insert. In dialogue Tanaka explaining to non-specialists, "mouth" is acceptable as metaphor — but we rejected it as stale once. Decide: is "mouth" allowed in dialogue but banned in proof inserts, or banned everywhere?
- **Recommendation:** allow in dialogue when a character is explaining to another character (Tanaka to Marshall, Tanaka to Nakamura). Ban in proof inserts and in neutral narration. Flag for consistent application across all dialogue.

**L845 (Tanaka to Nakamura at relay).** `"It is going to find a way whether we help it or not. All we can do is choose the terms."`
- **Note:** "find a way" is idiomatic human speech. In Tanaka's voice under stress it reads like character, not metaphysical claim. But strictly it implies the entity has a goal.
- **Recommendation:** tighten to eliminate the idiom. E.g., `"The pull continues whether we help or not. All we can do is shape how it arrives."`

**L565 (main revelation).** `"The thing that was already eating this region registered us as the calorie-dense part of the meal. It started pulling."`
- **Note:** "registered" is sensory-detection, not mental. OK in shark context, but flag for consistency. If any character's line uses "sensed" or "detected" instead, harmonize.
