### TANAKA flags

**T-1 & T-4 — Tanaka's knowledge of Nakamura's archive file.**
- **Problem:** At L254 (dinner, Day 47, long before Tanaka's revelation) he tells Nakamura IN JAPANESE to "open the file." By Day 54 (L716) he explicitly names it as "the file from three years ago." Nobody told him about it. The script implies the signal has given him knowledge about her.
- **Analysis:** This is consistent with the locked premise that Tanaka is being pulled and in the process the entity's proximity to his cognition gives him readings of other coherent patterns on the ship. But the mechanism is never explained in dialogue. The audience sees him know things he shouldn't know. This is good horror if the audience is allowed to infer it, but right now the inference is vague.
- **Fix:** One line somewhere that frames it. Could be Tanaka at L724 saying: "When you sit long enough next to this signal, you see what it sees. I saw your file." One sentence. Seals the mechanism.

**T-2, T-3 — metaphysics violations.** Addressed in metaphysics.md.

**T-5 — mouth language.** Addressed in metaphysics.md.

**T-6 — "find a way" idiom.** Addressed in metaphysics.md.

**Arc status:** **CLEAN** at the arc level. Tanaka's willing-one shape holds. Individual-line violations are fixable in Pass 1.
