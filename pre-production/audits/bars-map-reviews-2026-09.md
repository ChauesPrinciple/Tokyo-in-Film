# Tokyo After Dark — review research (September 2026)

What reviewers say about every stop on `tokyo-after-dark.html`, plus places where the page copy
disagrees with what reviewers or listings report. Everything below is paraphrased.

**How to read the ratings.** Tabelog runs low: 3.0 is average, 3.5+ is "very good," 3.8+ is
top tier. Google and Tripadvisor scores are not comparable to Tabelog's. A low review count
(under ~10) means the score says little.

Smaller copy notes (not wrong, but thin or unverified):

- **Apollo Bar (1):** reviewers describe a dim, classic New York-style room, not "modern." ¥1,000 cover.
- **Star Bar (2):** official name スタア・バー・ギンザ. ¥1,100 table charge.
- **Bar Cocktailbook (7):** the "bookshelf of recipes" detail didn't turn up anywhere. Reviews say the owner (Akira) is a cocktail-competition winner. 13 seats, ¥600 charge, closed Sun–Mon.
- **Bar rest (9):** no independent reviews found anywhere — only the bar's own site. Nearest station is Seibu-Shinjuku (1 min).
- **a BAR Sleeping Tales (11):** the page undersells it. No menu; the owner (Toshi) builds drinks from your preferences. Medieval-European décor. Closed Wednesdays.
- **A10 (15):** the entrance hides behind a row of green lockers. ¥1,320 charge.
- **JANAI COFFEE (14):** you solve a puzzle on the website to reach the online-only booking page, then go through a bookcase door.
- **SAKEBARO (30):** non-members can get in through occasional ticketed dinner services booked online; the address comes by email.
- **BAR COO (12):** sources say about five seats; the page says six.

## 2. Stop-by-stop

### Ginza & Nihonbashi

**1. Apollo Bar Ginza** — Tabelog 3.62 (56) · Tripadvisor 5.0
- Praise: speakeasy feel down a side alley; house-made tonic, juniper syrup and ginger cordial; excellent whisky highballs; good for solo drinkers.
- Caveats: ¥1,000 cover; typical spend ¥4,000–5,000.
- Practical: Mon–Fri 17:00–01:00, Sat to midnight, closed Sun/holidays.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1301/A130102/13160143/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g14129573-d9961227-Reviews-Apollo_Bar_Ginza-Ginza_Chuo_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)

**2. Star Bar Ginza** — Tabelog 3.78 (421)
- Praise: owner Hisashi Kishi (IBA world champion, government-honored master craftsman) and his Sidecar; hand-cut "ninja ice"; hushed, temple-like basement; off-menu drinks on request. Among the first Japanese bars on the World's 50 Best list.
- Caveats: pricey (budget ¥6,000–8,000); ¥1,100 charge.
- Practical: daily 17:00–23:30.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1301/A130101/13002270/), [PUNCH](https://punchdrink.com/venues/star-bar-ginza/), [50Best Discovery](https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/Japan/Tokyo/Star-Bar.html)

**3. Bar Lamp** — Tabelog 3.10 (19)
- Praise: relaxed "local bar" feel despite the Ginza address; rum and cigar focus.
- Caveats: few reviews; budget ¥5,000+.
- Practical: Mon–Sat 17:00–02:00; reservations by phone.
- Sources: [Tabelog (current)](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1301/A130101/13257552/), [relocation notice](https://www.bar-lamp.com/news/shopmove/)

**4. NOCTURNE** — Tabelog 3.03 (2), listing on hold
- No usable reviews. Last listed hours: Mon–Fri 18:00–02:00, Sat to 23:00.
- Source: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1301/A130103/13058173/)

**5. The Pizza Bar on 38th** — Michelin-listed; 50 Top Pizza World #2 (2025)
- Praise: eight seats with front-row views of the chef; light 48-hour-fermented crust; Fuji visible on clear evenings; polished Mandarin Oriental service.
- Caveats: very hard to book; expensive for pizza.
- Sources: [Travelsort](https://travelsort.com/review-pizza-bar-on-38th-mandarin-oriental-tokyo/), [Tokyo Shortlist](https://tokyoshortlist.com/tokyo/review-of-pizza-bar-on-38th-in-chou-city-tokyo), [eurweb](https://eurweb.com/pizza-bar-on-38th/)

### Shinjuku

**6. New York Bar (Park Hyatt)**
- Post-renovation reviews: the bar was deliberately kept as it was — same skyline, same slow jazz — with restored interiors, better acoustics and a new cocktail list that nods to *Lost in Translation*.
- Caveats: cover and hotel pricing (see table above).
- Sources: [Hyatt newsroom](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/120925-Park-Hyatt-Tokyo-Reopens-Following-19-Month-Renovation), [hotel bar page](https://restaurants.tokyo.park.hyatt.co.jp/en/newyorkbar/), [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/travel/hotels/park-hyatt-tokyo-reopening-studio-jouin-manku-interview), [Table Journey](https://tablejourney.com/japan/tokyo/bars/new-york-bar-park-hyatt/)

**7. Bar Cocktailbook Shinjuku** — Tabelog 3.12 (38) · Tripadvisor top 1%
- Praise: monthly seasonal cocktails; matcha Old Fashioned; the bartender pours side tastes of each spirit so you can compare; a calm "third place."
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1304/A130401/13132821/), [blog review](https://everybodyhatesatourist.net/a-perfect-cocktail-experience-at-bar-cocktail-book-in-shinjuku-tokyo-japan/)

**8. KAKURETAI** — Tabelog 3.03 (1); strong on Google and travel sites
- Praise: owner Yuta treats first-timers like regulars; whimsical mouse décor; seasonal-fruit cocktails and Japanese whisky; LGBTQ+-friendly.
- Caveats: small, fills up — reserve.
- Practical: Mon–Fri 20:00–01:00, closed weekends (matches the page).
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1309/A130903/13282157/), [Evendo](https://evendo.com/locations/japan/kai/bar/kakuretai-speakeasy-bar)

**9. Bar rest (snack bar)** — no third-party reviews found
- From its own site: window counter looks onto Seibu and JR trains passing about once a minute (after last train, maintenance vehicles appear). 7 counter seats plus a 6-person box; no food; smoking allowed; Tue–Sat 18:00–24:00.
- Source: [official site](https://bar-rest.shop/)

**10. SAKE MARKET Shinjuku** — Tabelog 3.33 (188) · Yelp 4.7 (18)
- Praise: great for sake beginners and foreign visitors; friendly English-speaking staff; bring-your-own food allowed.
- Caveats: self-serve format won't suit people who want guidance at a counter.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1304/A130401/13193816/), [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/sake-market-shinjuku-%E6%96%B0%E5%AE%BF%E5%8C%BA), [Laura's Suitcase](https://www.laurassuitcase.com/all-you-can-drink-sake-bar-in-tokyo/)

**11. a BAR Sleeping Tales** — Google ~5.0 (~175) · Tabelog 3.04 (3)
- Praise: one of the coziest bars in Shinjuku on social media; bespoke drinks (a peanut-butter Old Fashioned comes up often); owner-bartender performs without pretension.
- Caveats: one Tabelog reviewer found it less than the online hype suggested.
- Practical: 18:00–02:00 most nights (to 03:00 Fri/Sat), closed Wed.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1304/A130402/13298230/), [Nomax](https://nomax.app/tokyo/bars/a-bar-sleeping-tales/)

**12. Golden Gai trio**
- **Happy (Rockin' Soul Bar)** — Google 4.8 (20). Pick records from the vinyl wall (60s–80s rock and soul); cover about ¥900; disco night on the last Friday of each month. Sources disagree on staffing: one describes an all-female crew, a newer one names an owner, Sosuke. [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/6565756/happy-rockin-soul-bar), [byFood](https://www.byfood.com/blog/tokyo/best-golden-gai-bars)
- **BAR COO (空)** — Google 5.0 (124). Owner Miya speaks fluent English; homey snacks (miso soup, seaweed salad); regulars and travelers mix easily. Closed Sundays; come early. [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1354686), [Medium essay](https://pjsauer.medium.com/the-upside-of-covid-the-2020-tokyo-olympics-didnt-ruin-the-golden-gai-4d9bf82235f6)
- **OPEN BOOK** — Tabelog 3.31 (94). Signature lemon sour (brown-sugar shochu, house lime syrup, Hiroshima lemons); always packed, mostly standing room; ¥300 charge; 20:00–23:55 daily. [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1304/A130401/13194477/), [NiEW interview](https://niewmedia.com/en/specials/grandmarquee26856/)

### Shibuya, Ebisu & Meguro

**13. FIGHT CLUB 428** — Tripadvisor 4.0 · Tabelog 3.00 (2)
- Praise: cheap drinks; watching or joining friendly sparring after 18:00; easy to meet other martial artists.
- Caveats: it's a fight gym first; hours are irregular.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1303/A130301/13194634/), [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/restaurant/fight-club-428/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1066456-d14747495-Reviews-Fight_Club_428-Shibuya_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)

**14. JANAI COFFEE** — Tabelog 3.47 (225)
- Praise: the puzzle-and-bookcase entry; coffee cocktails; English-speaking staff; R&B playlist; decaf coffee handed out as you leave.
- Caveats: drinks ¥1,400–1,600 read as expensive; booking can be hard.
- Practical: 18:00–24:00 daily; three-cocktail course ~¥3,500.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1303/A130302/13249859/), [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/restaurant/janai-coffee/)

**15. A10** — Tabelog 3.58 (332)
- Praise: record bar with a DJ on vinyl (soul, R&B, jazz); high-end sound system; original cocktails overseen by mixologist Munenori Nagumo.
- Caveats: busy Fri/weekends — reserve.
- Practical: 19:00–02:30 daily.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1303/A130302/13262489/), [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/restaurant/a10/)

**16. BUNDAN** — Tabelog 3.52 (212)
- Praise: about 20,000 books to browse; drinks and dishes named after writers and novels; quiet patio by Komaba Park.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1318/A131801/13146659/), [tsunagu Japan](https://www.tsunagujapan.com/a-day-of-literature-and-art-in-komaba-park/)

**17. Bar SAWA (Nakameguro)** — Tabelog 3.09 (34)
- Praise: enormous rare-whisky shelf (Dalmore Sirius, 1948 Macallan); knowledgeable owner Shigeko Shintani, who has visited 130+ distilleries; warm yellow light outside.
- Caveats: actual spend often ¥8,000–10,000.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1317/A131701/13135861/), [Dear WHISKY](https://en.dearwhisky.com/news/bar-sawa-shigeko-shintani)

**18. No Room For Squares** — Tabelog 3.08 (20)
- Praise: entrance through a retro Coca-Cola vending-machine door; 1,000+ records on big speakers; no menu — the owner mixes to your taste; weekend live jazz.
- Caveats: tiny and loud during sets; cash only.
- Practical: 14:00–02:00 daily.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1318/A131802/13239103/), [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/restaurant/no-room-for-squares/), [Jazz of Japan](https://www.jazzofjapan.com/no-room-for-squares/)

### Minato & Roppongi

**19. Gen Yamamoto** — Tabelog 3.30 (67); a Tripadvisor and Yelp favorite
- Praise: one bartender, eight seats, weekly-changing seasonal cocktails built on Japanese produce; often called a highlight of a Japan trip.
- Caveats: small pours with little alcohol — some reviewers love the flavors, others feel short-changed.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1307/A130702/13154605/), [Savvy Tokyo](https://savvytokyo.com/cocktail-omakase-bar-gen-yamamoto/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g14129573-d4066967-Reviews-Gen_Yamamoto-Azabujuban_Minato_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)

**20. Bar Centifolia** — Tabelog 3.66–3.84 (~280; the score moved between checks)
- Praise: owner Yuzo Komai's drinks as visual art; care in every serve; memorable for a special night.
- Complaints: some call it Instagram hype — lots of theatre, not much drink — and very expensive.
- Practical: 18:00–03:00 daily; 14 seats.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1307/A130702/13213226/), [Tripadvisor reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g14129734-d27967542-Reviews-Bar_Centifolia-Azabujuban_Minato_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)

**21. Rokusan Angel (formerly Burlesque Tokyo)** — Tabelog 3.09 (43)
- Praise: energetic, weekly-changing themed shows (70–90 min); dazzling room; good for celebrations; performers interact with the audience.
- Caveats: book ahead for front seats; some language barriers.
- Practical: 18:30–01:00 daily.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1307/A130701/13136196/), [Rental Japanese review](https://rental-japanese.com/rokusan-angel-reviews/)

### East Tokyo

**22. BAR &UNDER** — about 4.0 on the former LINE Place listing (now on Yahoo! Maps)
- Praise: welcoming to people new to bars (crowd mostly 20s–40s); ~30 seats; creative sake cocktails from manager Koji Yamamoto; board games available.
- Sources: [BIMY feature](https://nihonshu.com/bimy/kaori/barunder/), [board-game bar listing](https://bodoge.hoobby.net/spaces/bar-andunder)

**23. NARU (Ochanomizu)** — Tabelog 3.47 (101)
- Praise: since 1969; ~30 seats packed around a grand piano; strong nightly working-musician bands; generous, good-value Italian-leaning food; English menu.
- Caveats: cramped for some; ¥2,500 cover plus drinks.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1310/A131002/13011591/), [Tokyo Jazz Site](https://tokyojazzsite.com/directory/listing/jazz-house-naru-ochanomizu-branch), [Jazz of Japan](https://www.jazzofjapan.com/naru/)

**24. Game Bar A-Button** — Tripadvisor 3.3
- Praise: a shrine of consoles and memorabilia; enthusiastic owner (ex-Sega); famous in gaming circles since 2008.
- Complaints: overpriced drinks; hard going without some Japanese; very small (13 seats).
- Sources: [igcc.jp feature](https://igcc.jp/barcade-01/), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1066461-d9948060-Reviews-Game_Bar_a_Button-Taito_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html)

**25. BAR Willow's** — Retty 83% positive (4)
- Praise: antique chairs; 200+ whiskies; cocktails from a former chief bartender at Yokohama's Royal Park Hotel; kind to bar first-timers; good non-alcoholic options.
- Caveats: best when quiet.
- Practical: Tue–Sat 18:00–03:00, Sun 17:00–23:00, closed Mon.
- Source: [Retty](https://retty.me/area/PRE13/ARE9/SUB901/100001542605/)

**26. Rock Bar Sakebi** — Tabelog 3.07 (10)
- Praise: pitch-dark room with odd lights; excellent sound; owner mixes rock with jazz, blues and techno; cheap drinks (budget ¥1,000–2,000); fine for solo drinkers.
- Practical: 10 counter seats; no reservations; closed Sun/Mon.
- Source: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1311/A131104/13237809/)

**27. The TRAD** — Tabelog 3.10 (27)
- Praise: European antique, castle-like interior; deep whisky shelf; cigars; relaxed for solo visits.
- Practical: 18:00–23:30 (to 24:00 Fri/Sat); smoking allowed.
- Source: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1311/A131101/13193086/)

**28. Eigakan Jazz** — Tabelog 3.09 (29)
- Praise: since 1978, run by a former film director and his wife; clear, full sound without being loud; reviewers call it a shrine to jazz.
- Caveats: cash only; smoking allowed; no reservations.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1323/A132301/13018624/), [Medium](https://medium.com/@heu-/sound-chronicles-eigakan-a914330ec3c8), [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2024/3/tokyo-jazz-joints-eigakan-jazz-kissa-project-info)

### Outer reaches

**29. Shooting Bar EA** — Tabelog 3.05 (12)
- Praise: 300+ airsoft guns on the wall; three 10 m lanes; staff careful about safety and good with beginners; gun-themed cocktails (the flaming SPAS-12 is the favorite); reasonable prices.
- Practical: reservations required; closed Tuesdays.
- Sources: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/tokyo/A1320/A132001/13136588/), [Stars and Stripes](https://www.stripes.com/living/after-hours-tokyo-s-ea-shooting-bar-mixes-guns-and-cocktails-safely-1.288621), [Hot Pepper](https://www.hotpepper.jp/strJ004004914/)

**30. SAKEBARO Aoyama** — no public ratings (members only)
- Coverage: tin-cube counter, self-playing tin piano, LED walls shifting blue to red, sommelier-paired pours; the tone is hushed and contemplative. Membership screening is strict; occasional ticketed dinners let outsiders in.
- Sources: [Tokyo Weekender (2025)](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/tw-collabs/inside-sakebaro-japans-most-mysterious-sake-bar/), [SAKEBARO](https://www.sakebaro.biz/)
