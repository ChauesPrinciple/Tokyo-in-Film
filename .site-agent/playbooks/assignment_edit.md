# Playbook: Assignment Edit

Covers the three journals, the phase guides (roles and Add Ons), the Documentary Project, worksheets in `guides/`, and printable field sheets.

## Read First

`../VOICE.md` section 3 (current film list); the page's row in `SITE_ATLAS.md`; the whole page.

## Steps

1. Ask which film(s) if the task does not name them.
2. New or rewritten assignments use only films on the current list. Films from earlier years already on a page stay unless he asks for the page to be rewritten.
3. Every task must be something a student can locate in the assigned film. Do not build a log or exercise around categories the film does not visibly show.
4. Do not tell students about the films. Direct them to what is on screen for the module's topic.
5. Do not ask students to reproduce material the page already supplies.
6. In-class worksheets: no exact timestamps; one log table per film; screening order (short films first, feature last); after the screening, pairs or small groups compare logs and argue to a position.
7. Take-home journals: exact single-moment timestamps in (HH:MM:SS); the 80-point evaluation stays as published unless he changes it.
8. Printed or downloadable student documents: 12 pt minimum everywhere, including tables.
9. Rubrics, point values, weights, due dates, and Google Form links change only on his instruction.
10. Run `python tools/check.py` and review the diff.
