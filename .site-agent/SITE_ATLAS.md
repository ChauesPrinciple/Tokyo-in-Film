## 8. The Documentary Project (`documentary-project.html`)

- **Subject:** one of two strands, A Place You Love or A Craft You Discovered. Both end in the same deliverables.
- **Teams:** groups of 3.
- **Shot recreations:** at least 7 from the 2026 film list, re-staged at real Tokyo locations: at least 2 establishing and 2 B-roll (cut-ins or cutaways), the other 3 from either category. Each has a one-page Shot-Match Sheet (original frame, title, timestamp; matching frame; composition analysis; one sentence on each shot's job).
- **Where to Steal From:** Ramen Heads, Jiro Dreams of Sushi, Tampopo, Exit 8, Your Name, Jujutsu Kaisen, 13 Assassins, Rurouni Kenshin: The Beginning, Rental Family, Outrage, Ringu.
- **Stages:** I. Pre-Production (group formation, greenlight pitch to the instructor, flagged shot list, interview questions). II. Production (10x footage to runtime, room tone everywhere, audio discipline). III. Post-Production (the (00:03:00) cut first, then the (00:01:30) cut from the same footage pool).
- **The Basics of Editing** (on this page): project setup, J and L cuts, jump cuts, cutaways, parallel editing, transitions, color and lighting continuity, audio levels, things to watch out for, free software (CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, ClipChamp).
- **Evaluation:** Shot-Match Sheets and recreation craft 30%; editing craft 25%; documentary voice 20%; process 15%; reflection 10%.
- **Submit:** both cuts (MP4), 7+ Shot-Match Sheets, pre-production pack, dailies and B-roll, self-evaluation naming the editing techniques used.
- **Collaborative requirement:** at least two hours assisting another group, with proof.
- **Extra credit:** behind-the-scenes reel.
- No deadlines are published on the page.

## 13. Website Style

**Theme tokens (`style.css` `:root`):** background #050505; text #e0e0e0; neon pink #ff007f; neon cyan #00f3ff; dark blue #0a0a23; card background rgba(255,255,255,0.03); border rgba(255,255,255,0.1); radius 8px. A faint seigaiha wave pattern sits behind every page.

**Type (locked):** h1 SDGlitch, uppercase, cyan glow; h2 Cinzel with a pink underline; h3 to h6 Inter 600; body Inter.

**Page skeleton (chapter):** `build:head` region, then `build:nav`, then `<main class="container">` with `<span class="toc-number">`, `<h1>`, and `<div class="textbook-content">` holding the numbered `<h2>` sections, then the quiz box where present, then `build:footer` and the scripts (`glossary.js`, `mobile-nav.js`, `youtube-facade.js`).

**Recurring components:** chapter banner video; LibreTexts video widgets (`mt-video-widget`) and lazy YouTube facades; `box-example`, `box-exercise`, `principle-box`, `example-box`; student quotes in blockquotes; `quiz-cta`; `assign-tabs` and `assign-panel` (phase guide roles); `guide-header`, `download-section`, `step-row` (worksheets); `film-slide` slider and `film-modal` (home); `.sheet` (printable field sheets).

**Code conventions:** static HTML with no framework. GSAP, ScrollTrigger, and Lenis from jsDelivr on the home page. Older scripts are ES5-style wrapped functions; the map engines are strict-mode modern JavaScript with explanatory comments. Cache busting is a content hash written by `tools/build.py`. HTML is UTF-8 without BOM.

**Build tools:** `python tools/build.py` (regenerate shared regions and hashes); `python tools/build.py --check`; `python tools/check.py` (freshness, regions, local links, mojibake, UTF-8).

## 16. Intentional, Do Not Change

- The phase guides and scaffold pages still name films from earlier years. Those assignments have not been rewritten for the 2026 list yet; Robert Ladd will rewrite them. Do not swap films on them unless asked.
- The home page Class Map is the Google My Maps embed on purpose, so students can get directions (decided August 30, 2026: class map is the Google embed, anime maps are `anime-map.html`). Do not replace it.
- Quotations (film lines, interviews, critics, students) are never reworded. An old contraction-expansion script altered some; restore only from the source.
- All footage for the class is shot in landscape. The Tokyo Reel is the one vertical deliverable, reframed to 9:16 in the edit for social media; it is not B-roll or a filming format.
- The site's address is chauesprinciple.github.io/Tokyo-in-Film. tokyoinfilm.com is not his domain.
- Do not state how a production was lit, recorded, or mixed (lighting ratios, binaural tracks, mic technique) without a source. Describe how the image looks or the track sounds instead.
- Tokyo Ghoul English dub: Austin Tindle voices Kaneki (not Bryce Papenbrook).
- Lens ranges site-wide: wide angle below 35mm, normal 50mm, telephoto above 70mm.
- Textbook chapters cite films studied in earlier years. That is correct; the course studied them.
- The standalone maps (ramen, Tokyo After Dark, Koenji, Otaku Japan) and the unlinked guides are unlinked from the course pages on purpose. Do not add links to them.
- The three field sheets in `guides/` are in progress. Edit them only when asked.
- Working-copy HTML and CSS files use Windows line endings (CRLF) while the repository stores Unix endings (LF). Git for Windows converts on commit, so this is normal. Keep each file's existing endings; read diffs with `git -c core.autocrlf=true diff` when working outside Windows.
- `index.html` and `textbook.html` duplicate the modules and film list on purpose.
- Em dashes inside original Sharman prose, and as separators in map labels, stay.
