Otaku Japan

The dream trip, compiled from a decade of saved pins. Akihabara for the shops, Ikebukuro for Otome Road, Nakano for the Broadway. Then the shinkansen west: Fuji-Q's Hidden Leaf, Ghibli Park, Kyoto's JoJo bar, Osaka's Denden Town, a day inside Super Nintendo World, and finally Awaji Island, where a park in the forest lets you zipline into Godzilla's mouth. Then home the smart way: bags couriered ahead, a short flight back to Tokyo, and one last shopping day, because you never shop at the beginning.
