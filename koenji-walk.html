Koenji Walk

Koenji has the densest concentration of secondhand clothing in Tokyo, about ten live houses within a few minutes of the station, and a postwar food market you enter through a greengrocer. This walk runs south from the north exit down Junjō, through Daiichi Market, along the Pal arcade into Look, and ends at a shrine — which one depends on where you want to drink.
