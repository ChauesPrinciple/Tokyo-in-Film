The 12 Bowl Ramen Challenge

Twelve bowls. Twelve neighborhoods. No two the same. Work through them in whatever order your feet and the trains allow, because this is a map, not a route. Then, if you think you have earned it, there is a thirteenth bowl waiting in Kanda.

  • 01 – 12

    The Challenge

    Twelve shops, twelve different bowls: shoyu, niboshi, shijimi clam, duck and leek, curry tsukemen, tantanmen, chicken paitan from the ramen kingdom of Yamagata, charcoal-grilled chuka soba, a four-sauce tsukemen counter, and one station basement holding ten shops of its own. Order the thing each shop is known for. That is the whole rule.

  • 13

    Bonus: The Oni Challenge

    Kikanbō in Kanda. Kara-shibi miso, which means chili heat (kara) stacked on sanshō numbing (shibi), and you set both dials yourself on a five-point scale. The top setting is oni-mashi. Devil level. It costs extra, and it should. (Come for my Oni level, if you dare.)

Before you go

  • Ramen Heads 2017

    Directed by Koki Shigeno

    Shigeno spends fifteen months with Osamu Tomita, a four-time consecutive winner of the TRY Grand Prize, at Chuka Soba Tomita in Matsudo. Tomita opens up the entire process, and when asked why some shops guard their recipes he says they are just not doing anything worth hiding. Watch it for the repetition: the same tasks, the same corrections, every single day. That is the part you cannot see from a stool.

  • Tampopo 1985

    Directed by Juzo Itami

    A ramen western, and the reason half the world thinks there is a correct way to approach a bowl. In the opening scene an old man narrates the whole ritual: study the bowl first, note the arrangement, stroke the surface with the chopstick tips, then apologize to the pork before setting it aside. It is a joke, and it is also completely sincere, which is very much the point. Do the ritual once at your first shop. See how it changes the second one.

Tokyo in Film Partner

Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum

Outside the thirteen bowls on this map, but part of the Tokyo in Film ramen story. The museum hosts a hands-on noodle-making class where you knead, cut, and boil your own ramen from scratch. We filmed the experience for a short piece on the project.
